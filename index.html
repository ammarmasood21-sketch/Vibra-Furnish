Our Heritage

The Past, Present, & Global Future

VibraFurnish started with a singular, radical vision: furniture shouldn't just occupy space—it should elevate consciousness. By combining vibrant color theory, custom ergonomics, and premium materials, we craft products that redefine home aesthetic experiences.

1998 — THE PAST

Bespoke Workshop

Started as a small guild of three master carpenters in Florence, Italy, specializing in customized aniline leather armchairs and handcrafted hardwood dining vectors.

2012 — THE TRANSITION

Vibrant Ergonomics

Pioneered custom orthopedic frameworks coated in extra-vibrant fabrics, integrating sound-absorbing contours to create individual quiet zones in busy offices.

2026 — THE PRESENT & BEYOND

Globally Operating in 45+ Countries

Now fully operating as a carbon-negative brand, delivering stunning modern furniture from automated smart facilities directly to homes worldwide in under 48 hours.
